Material Risk in Motion

Geopolitical Turmoil Keeps Material Risk Moving.

Metal-intensive programs carry several exposures at once. Price, origin, availability, freight, and release timing should be reviewed together—not as separate conversations.

01

Trade Actions

Tariffs, exclusions, and country-of-origin rules can change landed cost and the viable supply route.

02

Energy & Freight

Shipping disruption, energy movement, and insurance exposure can alter delivered cost and timing.

03

Multiple Alloy Inputs

HRC, aluminum, and copper do not move together. Mixed-material assemblies carry multiple market exposures.

04

Quote Discipline

Price basis, validity, escalation, substitutions, and release assumptions should be explicit and time-stamped.
