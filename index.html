01 / Overflow Base Fabrication

Orders Grow. The Assembly Floor Is Full.

Problem to understand
Welding bases competes with generator assembly for bays and people. Which fabrication hours and floor area are constraining output?
Proposed response
Move an approved base package to AWC, confirm capacity, inspect the first article and deliver lots to the agreed assembly sequence.
Intended customer benefit
More space and labor available for integration and testing, with fewer fabrication tasks to manage in-house.
How we would measure it
Assembly space released, internal hours reassigned and on-time lot delivery.
02 / Repeat Skid or Frame Program

A New Configuration Needs a Repeatable Supply Path.

Problem to understand
Mounting details, revisions and material availability can disrupt the first build. Which interfaces and dates drive acceptance?
Proposed response
Review approved drawings, flag fabrication and sourcing issues for your engineer, agree inspection points and prove one article before repeat releases.
Intended customer benefit
Fewer fit-up surprises and a clearer basis for price, acceptance and repeat delivery planning.
How we would measure it
First-article acceptance, fit-up rework hours and adherence to the agreed release plan.
